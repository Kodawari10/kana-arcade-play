❓ How to play

Kana are invading! Shoot each falling character by giving its romaji before it hits the ground. Each level unlocks a new kana row (from level 11, the が ぱ tenten & maru rows) and the invaders get faster. After a few levels, whole words drop in too. Chain hits for a combo multiplier!

Keep Kana Arcade as a little app: one tap to open, plays offline.
